How to use VeriHuman

Analyze audio

Open Detect Audio, choose WAV, FLAC, MP3, or OGG files, then select Analyze audio. The result shown is returned by the existing server-side model.

What does UNCERTAIN mean?

It means the model did not have enough confidence for a safe BONAFIDE or SPOOF decision. Review the recording and its report.

Is my audio stored?

No, not by default. You can explicitly enable private audio retention in Settings.
