Audio features used by the model

These signal-processing features describe voice characteristics, timing-related changes, energy, and spectral detail. The active feature set is shown above.

MFCC

Captures voice characteristics and frequency patterns.

Delta MFCC

Captures changes in voice characteristics over time.

Delta-Delta MFCC

Captures changes in those changes.

Spectral Centroid

Measures the brightness of sound.

Spectral Bandwidth

Measures the spread of sound frequencies.

Spectral Rolloff

Measures the frequency below which most spectral energy lies.

Zero Crossing Rate

Measures how frequently the audio signal changes sign.

RMS Energy

Measures audio energy and intensity.

Spectral Flatness

Helps distinguish tonal and noise-like sounds.

Spectral Flux

Measures changes in the spectrum between frames.

Spectral Contrast

Captures differences between spectral peaks and valleys.

Chroma

Captures energy distribution across musical pitch classes.

Inference workflow

The server decodes the recording, resamples it to 16 kHz mono through the existing audio pipeline, creates 5-second chunks, extracts the same 172-feature vector, runs the loaded classifier, applies the existing calibrator, and uses the configured thresholds.

No retraining, threshold tuning, or feature-pipeline changes are performed by the website.
