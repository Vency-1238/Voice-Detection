About VeriHuman

Trust every voice. Verify every sound.

VeriHuman is a focused interface for inspecting audio authenticity with the existing Audio Deepfake Detection model. The website adds secure accounts, history, and reports around that model without changing its prediction logic.

Responsible use

Treat every result as evidence for review, not a final decision about a person or recording.

Limitations

Phone recordings, music, heavy noise, and audio unlike the training data can be misclassified. UNCERTAIN means needs review.
